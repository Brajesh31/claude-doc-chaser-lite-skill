# Client Email Safety Guard

Run this checklist before returning any client-facing draft.

## Scope
- [ ] Exactly one client and one email are being drafted.
- [ ] The message is a routine document reminder, not a legal notice or professional opinion.
- [ ] No automated sending or external contact has occurred.

## Accuracy
- [ ] Every requested document appears exactly as supplied.
- [ ] The due date is copied from the brief, not inferred.
- [ ] The engagement type is not used to invent additional requirements.
- [ ] No receipt, filing, review, payment, or approval status is fabricated.
- [ ] No penalty, consequence, or escalation is invented.

## Privacy
- [ ] Only necessary client identifiers are included.
- [ ] Sensitive identifiers and financial details are excluded unless essential and explicitly requested.
- [ ] The signature uses only approved profile details.
- [ ] No other client's information appears in the draft.

## Tone and delivery
- [ ] The tone is respectful, neutral, and helpful.
- [ ] The requested next action is clear.
- [ ] The email is a draft for human review.
- [ ] Internal review notes are outside the client-facing email.

If any required fact is missing, stop and ask for clarification. Do not fill gaps from assumptions or previous client records.
